# rio
Río: a river of light and the sound of a real stream, running on its own. Tap Begin, and nothing else is ever asked of you.

The water sound is "Stream" by gluckose ([Freesound 333987](https://freesound.org/s/333987/)), dedicated to the public domain under CC0, by way of the [Blanket](https://github.com/rafaelmardojai/blanket) app. It is raised 13 dB and saved as MP3, and it loops through a four-second equal-power crossfade. Every tunable number sits at the top of `app.js`.
